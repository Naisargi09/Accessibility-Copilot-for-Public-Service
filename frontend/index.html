The Digital Barriers in Public Portals

Government digital portals are crucial, but millions of citizens are left behind due to technical and interface obstacles.

Complex Navigation

Overwhelming menus, deep links, and dense jargon make it highly frustrating for basic users to locate necessary certificate templates or guidelines.

Lengthy Forms

Applying for basic services requires entering repetitive fields with complex definitions and zero immediate feedback on error states.

Visual & Motor Inaccessibility

Lack of contrast, fixed small fonts, and strict mouse dependence lock out visually impaired citizens and seniors with tremors.

Who This Portal Empowers

Designed purposefully to bridge the digital divide for four key groups of citizens.

👴

Large fonts & Spoken instructions

🕶️

Key Accessibility Assistant Features

Four core subsystems engineered to deliver an inclusive, hurdle-free portal experience.

Interactive Voice Navigation

Complete page controls using natural spoken terms. Scroll up or down, jump to navigation links, and open specific forms hands-free.

Intelligent Text Reading

Instant audio narration. Hover over any text block or card, click a reading button, and hear instructions read aloud in English or Hindi.

Smart Form Assistant

Step-by-step guidance on complex forms. Validates input values, highlights errors visually, and guides users using clear spoken guidelines.

Bilingual Live Assistant

Chat-based guidance system answering queries for key certificates. Tap buttons, write questions, or dictate query topics in English/Hindi.
